Van Jones discusses bridging political divides at Charter Workplace Summit; new AI Pulse research finds only 1 in 10 AI users see major benefits

Van Jones spoke at the Charter Workplace Summit 2026 about how fragmented media feeds drive workplace political division. Separately, Charter's AI Pulse research found only about 1 in 10 AI users say the technology significantly improves both their work and lives, with agency over AI integration being the strongest predictor of benefit. Why it matters

The Verge AI

Blue Planet III keeps imperfect footage to prove it's real in the age of AI

BBC's Blue Planet III is deliberately keeping imperfect shots — plankton in the water, boats in frame, suboptimal conditions — that would have been cut in previous series. Executive Producer Elizabeth White says this proves the footage is authentic, since AI content tends to look overly polished. The series showcases real animal behaviors including dolphins using sea sponges as tools and inter-species fish communication.

Update

Why it matters

A striking admission that authenticity now requires visible imperfection — the 'uncanny valley' problem inverted.

TechCrunch AI

Block's Dessa voice model sounds eerily human, trained entirely on speech

Block released Dessa, a full-duplex speech-to-speech voice model trained entirely on human speech that produces natural laughs, pauses, and emotional expressions. It can run locally on consumer hardware like a MacBook M4 Max. Block is seeking safety researchers to test it before public release and sees potential applications in powering business and consumer voice experiences at lower cost.

Update

Where coverage differs: The Verge reports Dessa was compared against OpenAI's GPT-Live-1 with Block claiming it sounds more natural but demos showing occasional awkwardness; TechCrunch emphasizes the eerily human quality without noting awkwardness

Why it matters

Running a convincingly human voice model locally on consumer hardware is a meaningful threshold — it democratizes the capability while making safety review harder to enforce. The Verge AI

Pennsylvania signs law requiring disclosure labels on AI-generated ads

Governor Josh Shapiro signed the Artificial Intelligence Deceptive Advertising Act, requiring AI-generated advertisements to carry disclosure labels across all formats including images, video, audio, and text. Labels must match the ad's format. The law applies regardless of where an ad was created if it runs in Pennsylvania. Notable loopholes include subjective definitions of 'significantly modified,' carve-outs for parody and satire, and a narrow trigger limited to content creating 'false consumer perception' about products or services.

Why it matters

A meaningful first step, but the loopholes — especially the subjective 'significantly modified' threshold — may limit enforcement and invite creative compliance workarounds.

TechCrunch AI

AI agents that live in your text messages are a growing product category

TechCrunch profiles AI agents that operate through SMS, iMessage, and RCS rather than dedicated apps. Notable examples include Ask Proxy ($1/day, proactively anticipates needs), Caddy (turns scattered phone info into tasks via iMessage/RCS), and Comma (manages tasks across Signal, Telegram, and WeChat). Instinct is the highest-profile player at a $10 billion valuation after raising $1 billion. The shared premise: text an agent like a friend instead of downloading another app.

Why it matters

The messaging-native approach cleverly sidesteps app fatigue, but the category's long-term viability depends on whether these agents can maintain utility as phone OS-level AI assistants improve.
