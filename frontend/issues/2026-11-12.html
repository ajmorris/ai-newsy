Cyberattack on Fireworks AI exposes Notion customers' chat transcripts

Fireworks AI suffered a cyberattack where hackers breached its cloud provider to access a development system, exposing customer billing information and datasets. Notion disclosed that approximately 1% of its customers had chat transcripts from its Notion AI 'Insights' feature stolen. Notion disconnected from Fireworks on November 3. This is Fireworks' second breach in two months.

Why it matters

A second breach in two months raises serious questions about Fireworks AI's security posture and the supply-chain risks companies accept when using third-party AI inference providers.

Norwest raises record $4B fund to deepen AI investments

Norwest, backed solely by Wells Fargo, has raised its largest fund ever at $4 billion to deepen investments in AI across consumer, healthcare, and technology sectors. The 65-year-old multi-stage firm plans to invest across the US, India, and Israel, with focus on AI-native applications, physical AI, domain-specific models, agentic cybersecurity, and aerospace.

Why it matters

A $4B fund from a single LP like Wells Fargo signals that major financial institutions remain bullish on AI despite broader market turbulence.

The Verge AI

United Airlines blocks AI agents from booking flights on its platform

United Airlines announced it will not allow personal AI agents to book flights, citing privacy concerns, unintended purchase risks, and federal regulatory requirements such as passengers attesting their bags don't contain explosives. AI agents can still display flight options but cannot complete transactions. This mirrors similar moves by eBay and major banks to restrict unauthorized AI agent access.

Why it matters

Airlines and other regulated platforms pushing back against AI agents could significantly limit the practical utility of emerging autonomous assistants from Meta, OpenAI, and others.

Guardian AI

US workers winning fights to protect jobs from government AI adoption

Workers across the US are pushing back against government adoption of AI systems that could increase surveillance, inaccurately evaluate performance, or lead to layoffs. Oakland city workers' union voted 94.1% to authorize a strike on October 22, seeking protections to ensure workers won't be laid off or disciplined by AI. The broader trend shows US workers and unions gaining ground in securing workplace AI safeguards.

Why it matters

A 94% strike authorization vote specifically over AI protections marks a significant escalation in organized labor's engagement with workplace automation concerns.

Guardian AI

AI helps UK economy defy slowdown expectations

AI has helped the UK economy grow at its fastest pace in over two years, defying forecasters' predictions of a slowdown. Despite ongoing threats from a potential tech slowdown and rising energy bills, the economy continues to outperform expectations four months after Chancellor John Healey took office.

Why it matters

The claim that AI is materially boosting UK GDP growth is notable but the causal mechanism is vague, and the resilience may prove fragile heading into winter.
