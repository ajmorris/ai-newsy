More Australians to live into their 90s as life expectancy forecast to rise over coming decades

Australia's Treasury intergenerational report, set for full release on Monday, projects that life expectancy at birth will rise to 89 years for women and 86 years for men by 2066. The report also forecasts that Australian household sizes will shrink over the coming decades, while a growing proportion of the population can expect to live into their 90s. These demographic shifts carry significant implications for government planning around healthcare, pensions, aged care, and workforce participation over the long term.

Why it matters

Rising life expectancy is broadly positive news reflecting improvements in healthcare, nutrition, and living standards. However, it presents substantial policy challenges that Australia will need to address proactively. Longer lifespans mean greater demand on the pension system, aged care infrastructure, and healthcare services, all while the ratio of working-age people to retirees continues to decline.
