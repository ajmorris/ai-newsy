PrismML brings its tiny LLMs to Qualcomm-powered smart glasses

PrismML, an AI lab founded by Caltech researchers and advised by UC Berkeley's Ion Stoica, has developed a version of its tiny language models for smart glasses running on Qualcomm's Snapdragon chips. Showcased at Qualcomm's Snapdragon Summit, the 1-bit Bonsai LLM is a 2-billion-parameter model tuned for vision and language that can run locally on the Snapdragon AR1 Gen 1 Platform. PrismML's technology shrinks larger models by 4x while retaining nearly all performance on standard benchmarks, enabling real-time visual question answering on smart glasses without cloud dependency. The startup positions this as a privacy-friendly alternative to proprietary cloud-based AI, though no smart glasses actually running PrismML have been announced yet.

This is a meaningful proof point for on-device AI, but the gap between a summit demo and a shipping product is enormous.
