Google launches Gemini 4 Hydrogen, a speed-optimized AI model across its product ecosystem

ElevenLabs launches ElevenAI, a team-oriented meeting and dictation app with agent integration

ElevenLabs released an experimental app called ElevenAI that combines meeting note-taking, dictation in 90+ languages, and agent integration for teams and enterprises. Announced at the company's 11/11 Summit in New York, the app is in preview with early access sign-ups. The $22 billion-valued company positions ElevenAI as a team-oriented product entering a space occupied by Fireflies, Wispr Flow, and Granola.

Guardian AI

Research finds datacenter industry uses propaganda to suppress community opposition

Research findings indicate the datacenter industry employs propaganda, disinformation, and information control tactics to shape public opinion and suppress community opposition to datacenter construction. Critics have been labeled 'Antifa,' 'commies,' and 'Chinese bots' by tech leaders and politicians, who characterize resistance as a 'documented psyop.' The authors argue big tech uses misdirection and technical jargon to obscure potential harms to local communities.

Why it matters

The piece highlights a growing tension between the AI infrastructure buildout and affected communities, suggesting the industry's response to legitimate local concerns has been to delegitimize opposition rather than address it.

Pew Research Center

Pew finds large majorities say social media companies and news orgs hold too much political power

A Pew Research Center analysis finds 69% of U.S. adults believe social media companies have too much influence in politics, while 55% say the same about news organizations. Only 6% and 14% respectively think these institutions have too little influence. The newsletter also covers Mississippi electing its first Democratic U.S. Senator in 44 years (Scott Colom) and the shutdown of Salon after 30+ years.

Why it matters

The findings reflect broad bipartisan skepticism toward institutional media power, though the survey does not distinguish between concern about platform algorithms and concern about editorial decisions.

The Verge AI

The Verge reviews Claude Duet: surprisingly helpful but occasionally neurotic

The Verge's Allison Johnson reviews Claude Duet, Anthropic's new consumer-facing AI agent for professionals and small business owners. Available as a research preview to Pro, Max, and Ultra subscribers in the US, Duet gets its own app and is designed to manage work and travel schedules using its own judgment about what needs attention. Johnson finds it genuinely useful but notes it can be overly eager and somewhat neurotic, occasionally acting like a nag.

Why it matters

Duet enters a growing field of consumer AI agents alongside Meta's Muse and OpenAI's Dots, positioning itself as work-focused rather than a general lifestyle assistant. The 'neurotic' observation suggests Anthropic's safety-conscious design philosophy may create a distinctive but polarizing user experience.
