US midterm results threaten AI data center expansion as Big Tech plans $720B+ in spending

Meanwhile, Amazon, Microsoft, Alphabet, and Meta plan at least $720 billion in capex this year, mostly on AI, on top of over $1 trillion in future commitments — despite AI not yet yielding significant returns. Wall Street reacted with a sharp tech selloff, and midterm voters broadly supported protecting ratepayers from data-center-driven electricity cost increases.

Australia warned AI and datacentre expansion could become 'political kryptonite'

Australia has yet to see tangible benefits from the AI and datacentre boom, with concerns about copyright changes hurting creatives, special rules around power and water use, and worker displacement. Drawing parallels to US midterm results where voters turned against data center expansion, the article warns the Albanese government that it struggles to articulate how AI will benefit ordinary Australians and risks political backlash.

Why it matters

The warning is well-timed given mounting public skepticism, but without concrete examples of voter consequences in Australia itself, the US parallel may not fully apply to Australian political dynamics.

100+ groups across 15 European countries plan 'Stop Dirty Data Centres' protests

More than 100 campaign groups across 15 European countries are organizing two days of protests this Friday and Saturday under the banner 'Stop Dirty Data Centres,' calling for a moratorium on large AI datacentre projects and citing unaffordable environmental and social costs.

Why it matters

The scale and coordination of protests across 15 countries signals that opposition to AI infrastructure is becoming a pan-European movement, not isolated local complaints.

Documentary exposes Global South gig workers who train self-driving cars under precarious conditions

French filmmaker Nicolas Gourault's short documentary 'Their Eyes' profiles workers in Kenya, the Philippines, and Venezuela who perform data labeling and image annotation for self-driving car AI under low-paid, precarious conditions. The film argues that machine learning discussions often omit the cheap Global South labor that fuels them, perpetuating geographical inequalities.

Why it matters

The documentary adds a human dimension to an underreported aspect of AI development, highlighting how the benefits and costs of autonomous vehicle technology are distributed unevenly across the globe.

Ars Technica AI

Russian APT29 hackers exploited patched iOS and Android flaws to infect phones via compromised websites

Sekoia researchers uncovered a hacking campaign attributed to Russia's APT29 that exploited previously patched iOS and Android vulnerabilities to target website visitors. Attackers used JavaScript injection, proxy domains, and a supply-chain compromise of a Ukrainian analytics service. Targets included america250.org and a proxy of the Raffles London hotel near UK government buildings. The campaign deployed DarkSword, an iOS exploit kit linked to Turkish surveillance vendor PARS Defense.

Why it matters

The supply-chain compromise of a Ukrainian analytics service and targeting of sites near government buildings suggests state-level espionage goals, making this more concerning than typical vulnerability exploitation.

Charter

NBER paper argues NBA load management offers lessons for preventing workplace burnout

An NBER working paper argues that NBA load management — benching healthy players to prevent injuries — offers lessons for workplace leaders on managing burnout. The economists found load management effective in basketball and contend the same principle applies to knowledge work: strategically pulling back high performers before they burn out can increase long-term output even if it reduces short-term productivity.

Why it matters

The analogy is intuitive but the leap from physical injuries in athletes to mental burnout in office workers needs more empirical grounding to be actionable beyond the metaphor.

Box integrates Nvidia's Nemotron embedding models for faster AI agent content retrieval

Box announced Box Content Retrieval at its developer conference, integrating Nvidia's open-weight Nemotron embedding models to enable AI agents to answer queries four times faster with 20% fewer errors and 90% fewer tokens. Ars Technica AI

North Korean hackers steal $6.34 billion from Binance in largest-ever crypto heist

Binance disclosed that North Korean hackers (TraderTraitor) executed a months-long campaign culminating in the theft of $6.34 billion in cryptocurrency — the largest crypto heist ever. Attackers compromised engineers through a trojanized Homebrew installer and fake job interviews delivering zero-day malware, then pivoted via an internal AI tool. Binance has frozen or recovered $2.85 billion and says it has reserves to cover the loss. The FBI and TRM Security attributed the attack to North Korean state-sponsored actors.

Why it matters

The use of a compromised internal AI tool as a pivot point represents a new and alarming attack vector that should put every company deploying internal AI assistants on notice.

Guardian AI

UK parliamentary probe finds 'dangerous' dependence on China for defence and AI critical minerals

A UK parliamentary business select committee investigation found the UK is 'dangerously' dependent on China for critical raw materials essential to military, renewable energy, and AI sectors. MPs are calling for a 'critical minerals finance facility' to fund rare earth mines and diversify supply chains away from China.

Why it matters

The finding underscores a strategic vulnerability that has been known for years but remains unaddressed — the call for a finance facility is a concrete proposal but faces the same cost and timeline challenges that have stalled similar efforts elsewhere.

TechCrunch AI

YC startup Discovered Materials offers $1M for AI solutions to five unsolved materials science problems

Discovered Materials, a Y Combinator startup using AI to discover new chip materials, announced a $1 million challenge offering $200,000 each for AI solutions to five unsolved materials science problems including cuprate superconductivity and Density Functional Theory improvements. When frontier AI models were tested on these problems with days of autonomous compute, they made little headway, highlighting materials science as a frontier where AI has yet to break through.

Why it matters

The challenge is a clever way to crowdsource research while demonstrating the limits of current AI — the fact that frontier models failed with days of compute is a useful corrective to AI hype.
