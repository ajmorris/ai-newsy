Mathematician at Anthropic claims AI-assisted proof of the Riemann hypothesis

Levent Alpöge, a mathematician employed by Anthropic, announced a Lean-compilable proof of the Riemann hypothesis developed with significant assistance from Claude. The proof uses advanced techniques involving Galois representations, automorphic forms, and cohomology bounds. Several mathematicians, including MIT's Andrew Sutherland, have confirmed the Lean code compiles, though full human understanding will take time. Alpöge timed the announcement after Anthropic's IPO on November 19, 2026. Separately, Stanford postdoc Alessandro Masullo produced a purely human-written proof of the General Riemann hypothesis, announced by Stanford professor Ravi Vakil. The Riemann hypothesis, dating to 1859, is one of the Millennium Prize problems.

Researcher plants 2 million canary secrets into AI supply chain, exposes widespread privacy failures

Security researcher HD Moore planted 2 million unique trackable 'canary' secrets — AWS keys, SSH passwords, unique URLs, DNS names, and git references — into AI prompts sent through the inference supply chain of routers, gateways, and resellers like OpenRouter and Vercel. At least three AI hosting providers were found compromised by attackers siphoning prompts and reusing credentials; multiple resellers logged prompts and passed data to unauthorized parties; and multiple providers ignored Zero Data Retention privacy flags.

Why it matters

This is the most concrete evidence yet that the sprawling AI inference middleman ecosystem is a serious security liability. Organizations sending sensitive data through third-party AI pipelines should treat every hop as a potential leak point.

International talks on controlling autonomous weapons collapse after Russian objections

Week-long negotiations at the UN Review Conference on Conventional Weapons in Geneva failed to establish a mandate for a legally binding treaty on autonomous weapons, collapsing early Saturday morning after Russia's objections could not be overcome despite overwhelming majority support from other states.

Why it matters

The collapse leaves lethal autonomous weapons development in a regulatory vacuum at exactly the moment the underlying AI capabilities are accelerating. Without a treaty framework, norms will be set by whichever militaries deploy first.

MIT Tech Review AI

METR's updated AI benchmark reveals growing gap between verifiable and open-ended reasoning

AI evaluation nonprofit METR released an updated 'time horizon plot' measuring AI capability by the length of human-calibrated tasks models can complete. While AI continues improving exponentially on verifiable tasks like coding and math, it lags significantly on tasks requiring judgment and open-ended reasoning. No AI-designed evaluation was deemed useful by METR researchers, despite frontier models surpassing most humans at software engineering.

Why it matters

The update usefully punctures the narrative that AI progress is uniform across all cognitive tasks. The distinction between verifiable and judgment-heavy work has major implications for which jobs and workflows AI will actually transform near-term.

Spotify founder Daniel Ek backs AI researcher's fusion power startup Europa Fusion

Daniel Ek is backing Europa Fusion, a UK-based fusion startup founded in February by Nantas Nardelli, an AI researcher with experience at DeepMind, Facebook AI Research, and Microsoft Research. Nardelli left Pacific Fusion over disagreements about approach, believing fusion startups should invest more in simulation and data-driven methods. Europa Fusion will pursue a pulsed-power approach using massive, precisely timed electrical surges to compress fuel targets.

Why it matters

Nardelli's bet that AI-driven simulation can shortcut fusion engineering is a novel thesis, but it's unproven and the pulsed-power approach remains one of the least mature fusion pathways.

Trump threatens 'tremendous tax' on companies building AI data centers outside the US

President Trump threatened to impose a tax on companies building AI data centers abroad, warning that China would otherwise capture the market. He made the remarks at a press conference following a summit with Chinese President Xi Jinping and Russian President Putin. The proposal appeared off-the-cuff, with Trump admitting he hadn't discussed it with his own staff. TechCrunch AI

Space data centers face unique financing hurdle: chips in orbit can't be repossessed

Lenders typically use chips as collateral for data center loans, but chips launched into orbit cannot be repossessed. Lenders behind SpaceX's $40 billion chip financing deal requested no purchased chips be used for orbital data centers. Elon Musk claims space will be the cheapest place for data centers within two to three years, contingent on reusable Starship flights. Google may hold an advantage deploying its own TPU chips in space through Project Suncatcher.

Why it matters

The collateral problem is a genuinely novel financial constraint that could slow space data center development regardless of technical feasibility. Self-funded players like Google have a structural edge here over debt-dependent competitors.
