Datacentre projects could face fees for grid access to ease connection queue, Ofgem says

Ofgem, Britain's energy regulator, has proposed requiring datacentre projects to pay significant upfront fees or provide financial security (such as letters of credit, bonds, or cash deposits) to secure electricity grid connections. The move aims to address a major grid connection backlog, with 315 datacentres currently queuing to connect, representing 73GW of demand—nearly 30GW more than the UK's entire peak energy demand of 45GW.

Why it matters

This is a sensible and necessary proposal. Cyera agrees to acquire Oasis Security for $1B to safeguard proliferating AI agents

Data security company Cyera, recently valued at $12 billion after raising $600 million, has signed a letter of intent to acquire Oasis Security for approximately $1 billion, mostly in cash with some Cyera shares. Oasis Security specializes in non-human identities, primarily AI agents, providing cybersecurity software that monitors agent behavior and manages their access permissions. Founded in 2022, Oasis raised about $195 million from investors including Accel, Craft Ventures, and Cyberstarts. This is Cyera's third acquisition this year, following purchases of Ryft and Genie Security, as the company pursues a unified identity and data security platform. While Cyera has surpassed $150 million in annual recurring revenue, it remains far from profitable despite raising approximately $2.3 billion in total funding.

Why it matters

This acquisition underscores a critical and rapidly emerging challenge in cybersecurity: securing the proliferating population of AI agents that are gaining access to enterprise systems and data. As organizations deploy more autonomous AI agents, the attack surface expands dramatically, and managing non-human identities becomes as important as managing human ones.
